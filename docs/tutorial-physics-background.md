# Incoming physics background scheduling experiment

This increment extends PR45's projected incoming scheduling to operation8. It
retains the actual shared public worker and full private state, lifecycle,
record order, RNG order, append, outcome, release and progress tails. Outgoing
ball continuation and endpoint queries remain nominal work. No merge or release
clearance follows from these finite experiments.

## Selection and complete cost scope

Incoming contact can derive a launch using five fixed eight-iteration ratio
routines and one integer root, copy318 launch bytes, then advance the ball,
scene and clocks. The guarded contact domain limits the root to128 increments
per derivation (the generic unsigned-word routine can take256). Disjoint player
Y intervals establish at most one contact derivation before movement/ball.
Serve setup, attached phases and timed serve launches are excluded.

The complete admitted unit includes classifier, coherent beam/timer reads,
public budget1 worker, history save/restore, launch capture, append, outcome,
optional variant finish, owner release and progress-return handling. Full path
and dispatch caps are excluded. A released variant must have unequal path counts
after its single append, so terminal geometry comparison returns before scanning.
The chosen projected route is never changed during a flight. Refusal preserves
original nominal execution of that same next operation.

Outgoing ball steps lack contact/root work but can still finish a variant and
enter geometry comparison. Endpoint queries also have separate complete tails.
They are deferred to keep this extension small and its partition reviewable.

Both add2500CCK root service and2500CCK residual reserve. Physics therefore needs

A read-only word enables physics admission. Control and candidate are assembled
from the same source and differ only in that loaded word; their symbols and all
other loaded bytes must agree. The CPU proof initializes each side once from
complete actual recorded fixtures captured16 dispatches after incoming origin,
uses different working-state poison, and compares complete private states,
ordered events, paths and cursors at every public worker yield. Classifier domain
fixtures are separately labelled once-declared states, never fed into a running
trajectory. Native PAL/NTSC trials retain controls, cache snapshots, complete
owners, callbacks, stack, inputs/ACK, sprite publication and resume evidence.

Independent correctness and timing reviewers inspect source and retained raw
proofs before draft publication. ROOT alone owns builders/Core/emulators.
Canonical/history/cache writes, input sampling, phase/RNG/event order, presenter
latch/rendering, clocks, cancel/menu/resume/seek behavior remain acceptance gates.
A same-layout switch alone does not prove paired physical inputs or equal entropy:
comparison must verify captured seeds/origins or explicitly limit speedup claims.

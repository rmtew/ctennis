# Tutorial scheduling specification

This is the normative definition for the planned deadline-driven tutorial scheduler. **MUST** denotes a pass/fail requirement; **MAY** denotes an optional implementation choice. Requirement IDs remain stable when the document is revised. This specification authorizes documentation and review only. Runtime implementation, changed reserves and merge require the next decision gate.

The scheduler must service real input when needed and use the remaining time for exact prediction without sacrificing due visible work. It must reconstruct opponent incoming flight through actual edited human contact or miss, then the actual outgoing landing/net/out outcome. It must not make contact at the original recorded tick merely to shorten reconstruction.

## Time and ownership invariants

**S02 — One admission owner.** The native main-loop scheduler MUST own deadline derivation, remaining-time admission and service dispatch. It MUST preserve the continuous cascaded CIA clock, fractional phase and nominal callback entry/completion accounting. Preview receives a bounded job allowance, not authority to reset clocks or dispatch hardware/UI work. The presenter exclusively owns the guarded beam/COPJMP transaction and displayed-bank retirement. IRQ code MUST NOT run prediction or logical input sampling. Changes to ownership MUST be audited from emitted entries through all writes, including tails and indirect calls.

**S03 — Two clocks.** Simulation MUST retain approximately 59.923 nominal updates/s in both standards. PAL display frequency and NTSC display frequency remain separate. The current simulation intervals are `11838 + 14906/65536` E-ticks PAL and `11947 + 13180/65536` NTSC. Publication opens at line 253; the current last safe lines are 307 PAL and 257 NTSC, preserving the four-line end guard. Overdue callbacks MUST retain the baseline catch-up policy: account elapsed, subtract one nominal interval per dispatched update and carry the remaining lateness/fraction forward, with paired started/completed generation bookkeeping. Optional work is refused while overdue; do not skip or fabricate callbacks to regain apparent headroom. Any changed catch-up/display coalescing policy needs separate review. No scheduler may assume one nominal callback equals one physical field, change PAL simulation to 50 Hz, reset the epoch after expensive work or hide entry lateness. Legitimate latest-complete bank coalescing on PAL is distinct from a missed due publication.

**S04 — Deadline set.** At each public yield the scheduler MUST account current elapsed time and derive: the next nominal update/completion deadline `D_tick`; the next transport-service deadline `D_transport`; the next logical-input sample deadline `D_sample`; and, for due visible work, producer cutoff `D_ready` and presenter-service deadline `D_present`. Physical deadlines MUST follow coherent beam/field observations and the selected standard, including wrap. Initially the producer cutoff is before the target line-253 opening; a tighter cutoff needs a separately established producer/presenter bound. The current first safe-window attempt consumes the field opportunity even if empty/incomplete; after that attempt, a new complete scene targets the next eligible field. A target that arrives earlier in the safe window may commit only if that field opportunity remains unused. Overdue mandatory work is serviced and recorded; overdue optional work is not admitted.

**S05 — Admission equation.** An optional atomic chunk `J` MUST fit every deadline it can delay:

`now + B(J, class, standard) + T(remaining mandatory path, deadline) + M(class, standard) <= deadline`.

`B` is the approved conservative elapsed bound for that exact work class. `T` includes all still-required work on that path, including public ownership release, qualification, input, render/completion and publication/IRQ effects where applicable. `M` accounts only identified residual uncertainty and quantization. Independent deadlines have separate inequalities; overlapping work is not added twice within one path. Caller checks, clock reads and admission overhead are included. The equation MUST use nonnegative wrap-safe elapsed arithmetic with declared maximum comparison horizon. A smaller known chunk MAY replace a declined large chunk; absent any fitting supported chunk, return to mandatory service/idle rather than borrowing from a tail.

The original operation and slice counts remain safety ceilings until reviewed, not a time budget. A cheap input envelope and an expensive dispatch are not interchangeable cost classes. Re-sample time before every new public chunk. A monitor detects violations after a chunk but cannot undo an overrun; admission safety depends on bounds established before execution.

## Live input and presentation service

**S06 — Keyboard transport.** All physical keyboard bytes/releases MUST continue to be pumped while the tutorial is paused. Transport updates device/matrix state and acknowledgement independently from consuming logical actions. CIA keyboard interrupts are currently disabled. `game_poll_keyboard` uses `last_timer_count` for acknowledgement age: the scheduler MUST provide current accounted time, assert/deassert ACK with the protocol's minimum hold and meet the approved maximum service/ack gap. It MUST NOT use an old timestamp across prediction chunks, busy-wait for ACK, assume an unpumped key is absent, or gate transport on the current menu's relevant keys. The existing 70-E-tick release test is a baseline implementation fact, not a newly proven protocol upper bound.

**S07 — Logical sampling and edges.** Physical joystick sampling, keyboard merge, UI edge derivation, core pad/result commands and tutorial controls MUST remain once per original nominal sample by default. Preserve held/released state, independent aliases, entry-held suppression, source hints, action edges, double-tap/menu gestures and held movement repeats. Repeated keyboard transport pumps MUST NOT repeatedly clear/consume UI edges or generate additional simulation envelopes. An unchanged-matrix fast path MAY skip demonstrably redundant scans, but MUST still process release/filter changes and due repeat/gesture clocks. Earlier urgent cancel/resume sampling requires an explicit bounded pending-intent accumulator, one-time consumption and reviewed behavior; it is not achieved by repeatedly calling today's edge-clearing `ui_sample`. Press/release transitions supported by the approved transport/sample contract MUST not disappear or be consumed twice.

**S08 — Presenter pumping and latch.** Reducing live presenter polling MUST preserve visible-phase rearming, at most one publication per physical field and correct handling of complete banks before/after the field opportunity is consumed. Current `blank_seen` is set before readiness testing, so an empty/incomplete first safe-window attempt consumes that field. Preserve this opportunity rule initially. Allowing a later ready bank to retry in the same field is a separately reviewed latch change, requiring retirement, exact beam-guard and one-commit-per-field proof; it is not implied by removing redundant polls. `blank_seen` clears on a visible-line observation; the line-253 IRQ alone never provides that observation. Retain current polling until a bounded visible/blank observation policy is proven. IRQ and main-loop pumps MUST share the same latch/transaction; duplicate notifications cannot cause a second COPJMP. Presenter critical sections and coherent beam rereads belong in elapsed bounds. A closed/missed window retains the immutable front bank and records a missed due handover; it never relaxes the guard.

**S09 — Due service and responsiveness.** Before and between bounded prediction chunks, the scheduler MUST service due transport/presentation and process input at its declared sample boundary before choosing more work. The approved transport gap `G_transport` and logical response limit `L_input` MUST include worst nonpreemptible work, service tails, entry lateness and interrupt interference. Neither may be derived from a mean callback. Continuous typing, simultaneous keys, aliases, joystick releases and cancellation during a longest admitted chunk must satisfy the declared contract. No recursive service call may enter a private simulation owner. IRQ presentation is the exception: it may commit only an already complete immutable bank and MUST not inspect private work.

## Exact simulation and generation ownership

**S10 — Original operations.** Recorded pad/result/round-poll operations are simulation envelopes, not live hardware polling. Every original envelope MUST execute in its recorded order. Only the approved human fixed-X/Y and held/released action override for the selected alternative may replace the corresponding branch origin/controls; all other recorded arguments, seed/policy, edge semantics and cursor positions remain retained; synthetic continuation MUST retain the original poll/pads/result/tick semantics. Preserve per-envelope contact/outcome/lifecycle checks and all ordered outputs. Replay never reads live hardware or continually injects entropy. A grouped packet MAY reduce scheduling overhead; it MUST NOT skip no-op-looking bodies, replace physics, assume every recorded prefix is regular, or postpone a required check until the packet end. Unsupported prefixes require the exact conservative path.

**S11 — Exact incoming/contact/outgoing.** Prediction MUST use the same actual core as the native game and standalone proofs. Human placement/action edits can change contact eligibility/time or produce a miss. Preserve both receiving ends, setup/wait/timed-serve handoff, player-phase contact gates, courtY/scene mutations, low-height/action/RNG decisions and serve/net/out/fault outcomes. After actual human launch, existing original ball continuation stops at its defined landing/net/out; no next opponent response is introduced. A later full318 is not reusable by merely patching X/Y: AI, RNG-call order, scene/input/audio/lifecycle dependencies differ. Only a context-qualified immutable actual origin may be reused. Any future projected filter needs a separate guarded equivalence proof and is outside these stages.

**S12 — Private state.** During work, the active match's complete canonical 318-byte state, history store/selection and public metadata MUST remain isolated, including audio/control/scene fields and out-of-state writable globals. Public preview yields restore its saved 72 metadata bytes; private contexts persist without copying them into the canonical match. Seek has its distinct selected-state rollback contract. Only allowed private buffers and explicitly declared native scheduler/UI metadata may change. Programmatic writable-global/bus-write audits MUST reject unclassified stores, not rely on the existing narrow trajectory hash. Different initial/poisoned working state must reconstruct identical complete state and ordered outputs.

**S13 — Generation and retirement.** Every prediction, endpoint, raster job and ready marker MUST carry selection and generation identity. Initial entry freezes the chosen match/history identity before requesting work and publishes a coherent waiting/placement state. A new edit retires old endpoint/ready/raster ownership, increments or otherwise uniquely changes generation, and coalesces latest placement at the original repeat cadence. Generation exhaustion/wrap MUST not permit old identity reuse: reject/retire outstanding work or prove a safe epoch transition. At a public yield, process changed input and validate identity before additional work or publication. Stale calls and duplicate commit/cancel/retirement requests make no canonical/history/public-output mutation; repeated valid step calls intentionally advance distinct work at their recorded cursor, not the same envelope twice; cancellation never restores an older selected state. A partial scene/target is never made ready.

**S14 — History, lifecycle and resume.** Preview MUST remain excluded while seek is pending or ready; seek admission, commit/cancel, history mutation/eviction and title/init/selection reset retire dependent preview identities. Seek uses its bounded existing API, not a synchronous seek hidden in a tutorial callback. Preview never crosses an unsupported retained lifecycle reset. Audio sequencing, AV_DONE continuation gates, gameplay clocks and scene-dependent gameplay fields remain actual core state; removing renderer-looking simulation work is prohibited. Actual sound/DMA sinks remain suppressed during isolated reconstruction. On resume/title exit, cancel background ownership and stale publications before the next live dispatcher, preserve input entry filters/defer behavior and restore the documented latest/selected match boundary exactly once. No extra dispatch occurs merely because prediction finished.

## Readiness, scheduling order and fairness

**S15 — Independent readiness.** Endpoint readiness, dense sample availability, visible-marker readiness and final two-alternative result readiness MUST be represented separately. A valid accepted bounded query from an immutable actual launch may publish the selected endpoint while its dense continuation is incomplete. The existing short sequential prefix remains; short flights finish without an unnecessary query. A rejected/ineligible query resumes exact sequential work with no synchronous fallback or repeated unbounded query retry. LIMIT is incomplete, and a miss/no-contact must not invent an outgoing landing marker. Generation-owned first actual COPJMP establishes publication, not an API return, a counter or later readback phase. Completing one alternative does not imply both results are READY.

**S16 — Priority.** Select eligible work at each public yield in this order:

1. Mandatory transport/presentation service, due nominal input, cancellation/resume and completion of callback bookkeeping.
2. Due visible placement, selected ready endpoint/marker and already-due selected normal-speed samples. A query cannot displace these mandatory tails. Pending menu/placement rendering has its own admission.
3. Selected endpoint prerequisites (resolve/PRIME/actual incoming contact), then its eligible bounded endpoint query.
4. Selected dense sample lookahead sufficient for the next due playback interval.
5. Other-alternative work and nonurgent trail/footer raster, under the fairness rule below.

A dirty optional footer cannot consume a reserved sample/marker tail merely because it was queued first. Menu-open policy continues to suspend prediction. Selected endpoint work stops being high priority once ready/terminal; repeated eligibility probes cannot monopolize future cycles. If currently supported cost classes cannot safely finish mandatory visible work, report blocked admission/missed readiness honestly; do not label the path complete or claim no drops.

**S17 — Playback and fairness.** Background computation MUST NOT accelerate visible motion. Preserve actual samples, existing qualification and normal-speed animation cadence: advance the dense sample index by two every two nominal callbacks, clamp at the last available sample and retain the nominal animation cursor to avoid drift. Selection switches reset that variant's visible sequence/readiness according to the existing UI contract; a switch alone does not authorize restoring or editing the frozen match. At an unavailable next sample, hold the last actual sample and indicate waiting; do not extrapolate or treat the available prefix as terminal. Terminal dwell/repeat requires the real completed outcome. Current code times 30 nominal callbacks from terminal preparation; the planned visible dwell must be verified from actual terminal COPJMP using an approved `D_dwell`, so delayed preparation/publication cannot silently consume the intended visible hold. Changing the dwell origin is an explicit G3 presentation refinement, not claimed existing behavior. Already-due playback outranks speculation; a complete terminal outcome is required before dwell/repeat. Lookahead uses an approved finite waterline `W_samples`; once met, eligible lower-priority work receives residual slots rather than unlimited selected precomputation. For a stable generation and selection interval with sufficient admitted capacity, both alternatives and optional bounded jobs MUST eventually progress. Define/test a maximum eligible residual-service delay `F_residual`; it applies only while known-cost work fits after mandatory duties and selected readiness/lookahead, not during sustained edits, menus or overloaded mandatory work. The manifest MUST define eligibility and age precisely (count/time of opportunities in which the next approved chunk fits), reset age only on progress or retirement, and distinguish ineligible wall time. Age eligible lower-priority jobs to prevent footer/trail starvation within that condition. Insufficient capacity is explicitly reported, not fixed by dropping samples or relaxing a deadline.

**S18 — Atomic chunks and safe fallback.** Execute original envelopes atomically; allow no hardware/input reentry or arbitrary preemption inside actual bodies. A chunk MUST have an approved bound for every possible path under its declared class, and stop at a public owner-release boundary. The current API may execute subsequent original operations after detecting launch inside one call; reserve the entire possible call including that transition. A future early-yield owner/packet must preserve the same branch state/events/cursors and establish its own bound. No class uses the remaining-time check alone as its cost proof. If a bound is unavailable, admit only an independently supported conservative class or no optional work. Retained baseline reserves are prototype fallback policy, not a proof of unknown work. An observed budget violation fails its gate and disables the affected optional policy; safe service continues without publishing partial work.

## Cost establishment and unresolved parameters

**S19 — Proof versus observation.** Fixed operations do not imply fixed wall time. 68000 multiply/divide/shift operands, conditional paths and variable loops affect CPU time; chip DMA, memory/bus arbitration, IRQ phase/entry/tail, masked intervals and coherent timer/beam retries affect elapsed time. The allowed timing model MUST state CPU/OCS clocks, memory placement, enabled DMA/sinks, video modes, interrupt sources/rates, keyboard protocol and OS/takeover assumptions. An assumption not enforced by the product is not available to a bound. A largest observed cost plus margin is a measured hypothesis, not proven WCET.

Establish `B` by emitted-code path/data/loop analysis with conservative hardware/interference assumptions, then challenge it with native operand, phase and transition coverage. Exhaustive finite domains should be enumerated where practical; larger domains require reviewed partitions and a conservative bound for all unenumerated members. Coherent reread loops need an explicit timing/model bound; marking them constant is invalid. Include wrapper validation, private release, request/PRIME/cancel and expensive transition paths. Prefer already-observed bus-inclusive timing for comparison, without adding its nested child time again. Any CPU-only analytical bound must explicitly add allowed bus/IRQ interference. The presenter ISR bus span excludes interrupt entry/final tail, which must be accounted separately if needed.

**S20 — Units and margins.** Admission uses E-clock ticks with upward rounding when converting costs. Current 68000/OCS relationship is five CCK per E-tick; verify the emitted counter/target relationship. Retained CCK-to-seconds rates are 3,546,895 Hz PAL and 3,579,545 Hz NTSC. Simulation fractional intervals are not rounded away. `M` MUST itemize timer-read uncertainty, rounding, measurement boundary omissions and model allowance; it cannot be an arbitrary percentage used to disguise an unbounded path. Do not subtract independent maxima to manufacture headroom. Unknown or violated assumptions invalidate the associated bound and block that policy's release.

No new numerical value is approved by this specification. These parameters MUST be recorded in a versioned cost/policy manifest tied to exact product/tool hashes, with status (proven bound, observed hypothesis or unsupported), allowed class and acceptance scope. Experimental calibration/diagnostic operation may use explicitly labeled conservative hypotheses, but MUST NOT mark S05/S18 deadline safety or G2 safety acceptance passed. Such classes remain unsupported for normative safe admission even if finite captures have zero misses. Release of this scheduling policy requires all safety-critical parameters resolved. A universal no-frame-drop claim requires conservative bounds covering every allowed path/phase and mandatory demand, not merely finite tests with zero misses. Otherwise report only zero observed misses for the named finite workloads. No Copperline evidence establishes a fresh WinUAE or physical-hardware pass.

## API and implementation gates

**S21 — Small first step and selected-first stage.** Existing `game_preview_step(generation, budget 1..4)` already runs original bodies, checks each envelope and releases private ownership. Time-based native admission can reuse it; a new packet ABI is not necessary for the first stage. Current `game_preview_desired_variant`, PRIME ordering and `game_preview_finish_variant` assume held then released. UI selection currently influences presentation/query, not all computation. Full S16 selected-first behavior therefore requires a separate planned owner-policy change with independent per-variant progress/completion and a deterministic legacy default. It MUST not falsely mark both complete when the selected released variant finishes first. Scheduling order may change between branches; RNG/state/event order within each branch must not.

**S22 — Background work.** Background prediction MUST be enabled only during the frozen active tutorial, with menu closed, no pending/ready seek, no resume/title/entry ownership transition and matching generation/selection. Otherwise it is disabled. Begin with background prediction after `complete_update`, retaining existing callback rendering and presenter fallback. No private owner persists into a live sample/dispatch. Background rendering is a later gate: `complete_scene`/`complete_update` currently tie readiness to callback generation; an explicit audited producer-completion owner is required before rendering between callbacks. Front and ready banks stay immutable; superseding/retirement occurs only under current ownership rules. IRQ may publish an earlier completed bank while a new private producer runs. Gate acceptance MUST distinguish that legal handover from exposing the current incomplete producer.

The user resolves interaction/capacity decisions and approves runtime scope. A failing gate pauses that increment; do not expand into an unrelated architecture rewrite.

## Requirement-to-verification contract

**S23 — Logical completeness.** Proofs MUST execute the actual emitted/shared core, not a separate model. Compare every reconstructed tick/envelope and every checkpoint-to-target boundary with an uninterrupted actual-core reference using the same edited initial origin, branch inputs and entropy policy: complete318, ordered outputs, cursor/phase/count/outcome/launch identity and history metadata. Exercise different/poisoned working state, all retained seek positions in the named fixture, budget 1/2/3/4 groupings, interrupted/repeated calls, variant interleavings and stale/cancel/retention-wrap flows. Exhaustively enumerate bounded API validity/generation/ownership cases where practical; declare remaining state/path partitions and guards. Preserve the independent 10,958-tick recording/seed as the separate unedited discovery/replay baseline rather than regenerate its expectations. Equality is per branch to its matching reference, not between different edited branches or to an untouched match state. Per-fixture exhaustiveness is not exhaustiveness over all tennis matches.

**S24 — Native timing/publication.** Focused future timing checks MUST use physical input and production scheduling, not an injected callback regime. Include both standards, both receiving ends, initial entry, held/fresh edits, key/joystick release, simultaneous aliases, variant switches, short/rejected query, contact/miss, serve/fault/net/out, audio/scoring/lifecycle expensive paths, seek begin/commit/cancel, resume and dirty menu/footer. Cover exact publication boundaries (253, last safe, last line), empty first-window attempts and ready completion after that attempt, visible-line rearming without requiring a line-0 observation, ACK assertion/minimum-hold/release and 16-bit ACK-age wrap, timer/cursor wraps, continuous typing and simulation/display phase drift. Wrap fixtures MAY initialize once at a declared boundary and MUST be labeled declared rather than natural-wrap evidence; no intermediate expected state injection is permitted. Declare finite samples/phase partitions and any unsupported class; unsupported classes retain conservative fallback and cannot inherit a bound.

Pass requires every admitted chunk fit its predeclared bound and every complete callback meet its absolute nominal deadline including entry lateness; every supported transport/ACK gap and response meet its limit; and every due eligible scene commit within its declared field/window. Verify actual COPJMP, completed generation/bank bytes, sprite headers, scanout pixels and DMA ownership; counters alone are insufficient. Playback advances only by its actual available sample/cadence and never substitutes endpoint coordinates as dense samples. Retaining front when nothing new is due is legitimate; retaining it past a due guaranteed handover is a failure. If prerequisites are unavailable under sustained edits/overload, report waiting/capacity failure distinctly, and do not claim a fulfilled no-drop/latency guarantee.

Differential performance comparisons use the same recorded controls and seed; independent live-entropy captures cannot establish a paired speedup or paired randomness after branch RNG-call divergence.

**S26 — Evidence and resources.** Every acceptance receipt MUST name requirement IDs, cost-manifest version, exact source/product hashes, target/standard, tools, consumed inputs, workload/sample/phase extent, outputs and failed/skipped controls.

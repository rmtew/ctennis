# G2 experimental cost policy

This policy admits only a single ongoing incoming projected preview envelope between nominal callbacks. It is a calibration prototype; S05/S18 and normative G2 deadline safety remain unsupported. No cost value below is a proven WCET, transport protocol limit or approved interaction requirement.

The root loop accounts time before keyboard ACK pumping, pumps the existing presenter, accounts again, dispatches an overdue nominal callback with the original fractional/catch-up policy or offers one background chunk, then returns to root mandatory service. Physical/logical sampling still occurs only in the nominal callback. The new owner cannot draw, query endpoints, complete a scene, alter the presenter latch, or advance either clock artificially.

Classes: ongoing HELD/RELEASED projected incoming routes only; no launch, READY, PRIME, RESOLVE, renderer/seek/transition, dirty placement/footer or due animation. Complete public budget1 operation3 pads,4 all-zero result,5 clear inputs and9 latch are admitted. Operation7 poll additionally requires private initialized PLAYING, no restart/core command/entropy policy and no score-mode bit5, so its actual round body follows the no-op return partition. Dispatch8, ball continuation, final geometry and all other opcodes retain legacy nominal scheduling. The next retained/synthetic envelope is peeked without mutating public72 or taking private ownership. Every retained body, argument, per-envelope check and synthetic poll/pads/result/tick order stays intact.

Prototype allowances in E-clock ticks: chunk1000, root mandatory transport/presenter service500, residual margin500, next full nominal callback11200, callback entry lateness350 and completion residual uncertainty250. At every admission, remaining=interval-phase after fresh coherent timer accounting must cover chunk+service+margin=2000. The next whole interval must independently cover fullcallback+entrylateness+completionuncertainty=11800. A callback waits for nominal entry; earlier idle slack is never borrowed for its completion. An earlier sample-entry cutoff preserves the existing nominal boundary; the completion inequality includes the full fresh-input/transition callback hypothesis. It is deliberately independent of the opcode count.

A coherent beam observation supplies the initial line253 presenter approach cutoff: exclude the current line and assume226CCK per remaining complete line, requiring (252-line)*226 >=10000CCK, and refuse bottom-window/wrap work. This is a conservative-looking hardware hypothesis requiring emitted/provider/manual review; it is not a physical-machine proof. Ready banks retain the existing first-attempt field latch and line253 IRQ. No earlier producer or tighter publication policy is introduced. Unsupported producer/lifecycle classes remain in baseline callbacks; their full S04/S24 guarantees remain open.

The new exact owner, complete public worker/release/progress tail, root service and fresh callback are calibrated in PAL/NTSC. Prior observed multienvelope cheap public spans below2651CCK motivate1000E(5000CCK), but do not establish it. Measurements must reject a chunk exceeding the declared hypothesis or a due callback overrun. Account/beam coherent-read retry, chip-DMA/IRQ interference, whole mandatory paths, transport buffering/ACK maximum, L_input and producer windows remain unresolved normative assumptions even when finite observations pass. No universal no-drop or paired CIA-entropy speedup claim is permitted.

This G2 partial prototype preserves held-then-released computation; selected-first/fairness, urgent logical sampling, background rendering and scan fast paths remain separate gates. Resource/release/appearance/WinUAE/hardware acceptance also remain open.

The versioned machine-readable hypotheses are in `tutorial-deadline-cost-policy.json`. Each receipt binds that file, scheduler/main source and executed native image hashes alongside compiled manifests and actual tool hashes. Observers measure accepted and declined root owners separately and the complete root account/keyboard/presenter/account span. A finite root-service comparison is reported independently; admission arithmetic/beam observations are not independently reconstructed. Consequently these observations calibrate selected paths, not every policy allowance.

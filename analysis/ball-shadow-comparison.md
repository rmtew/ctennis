# Ball and shadow spacing in the original cartridge

The black two-by-two dot is source sprite record 9 at `$C034-$C037`: it follows the ball's court-plane position. The white ball is the active record selected among 0, 4 and 8. Both use the same X in the retained captures. The Amiga integration probe maps the two source Y attributes with the same vertical offset, so their separation in output pixels is twice the separation in source pixels.

In the no-serve movement capture, the ball bobs above the lower player while its shadow stays at source Y `$B4` (180). Across frames 1300-1339, the recorded ball/shadow Y separation ranges from 20 to 36 source pixels. At frame 1320 the RAM records ball Y 145, shadow Y 180 and the same X 182. A new original-cartridge MAME screenshot, `build/reference/source-serve/ball-shadow/ball-shadow-wait-1320.png`, shows white ball pixels at screenshot Y 157-158 and black dot pixels at Y 193-194, a 36-pixel top-edge separation. In the Amiga integration screenshot, the white ball begins at Y 306 and the black dot at Y 376, a 70-pixel separation at roughly twice the display scale. These are different update samples but consistent with the source's bobbing gap.

The actual serve/flight capture behaves differently: the source ball and shadow attributes meet at Y 77 on frames 1361-1362 and at Y 160 on frame 1411, with matching X each time. The original-cartridge screenshots for frames 1350, 1361, 1390 and 1411 are under the same ignored `ball-shadow/` directory. Thus the large gap is present in the original pre-serve animation; the shadow meets the ball at the court-plane crossing during flight.

`scripts/capture_source_ball_shadow.lua` reproduces the screenshots in MAME 0.289 SC-3000 using the same one-player selection and input timing as the retained captures. Set `CT_BALL_SHADOW_SCENARIO=wait` for frames 1310 and 1320 with the timed left input, or `serve` for the four serve/flight frames; run with the locally configured G-1009 `champtns` cartridge and `-snapshot_directory build/reference/source-serve/ball-shadow`. The snapshots remain ignored build evidence. The source coordinates come from `build/reference/source-timing/run_a.tsv` and `build/reference/source-serve/run_a.tsv`.
